BM26 · Titanic's End · global effects audit

Global Effects Gallery

Every card is the SAME deterministic 12-second sequence on the full 964-pixel Titanic model: 2 s of the untouched base pattern (baby_tease/01_bullseye_tide, saved playlist defaults), the effect ON from 2 s, OFF at 8 s, then 4 s of tail so release behaviour is visible. Trigger effects fire at 2 s, 4.5 s and 7 s. The chain is engine.js's own order — applyPixels → applyMacros → applyInvert → applyPostInvert — with a synthetic steady 120 BPM clock and NO audio.

W/A/U visualization. W/A/U lanes are composited into the rendered RGB for display only: rgb += w*[1,0.92,0.75] + a*[1,0.55,0.10] + u*[0.45,0,1], clamped to [0,1]. All manifest metrics are measured on the raw six-lane output BEFORE this mapping.

Metrics. Δ RMS mean per-frame six-lane RMS byte delta vs the base capture during the ON window · % px mean fraction of pixels differing from base during ON · peak × peak frame brightness (post-visualization) over the base peak, the flash-hazard number · tail seconds after OFF until the RMS delta drops below 2 bytes · ms/f mean wall time for the whole effect chain on 964 pixels. W/A/U ONLY marks a preset whose RGB is untouched and whose white/amber/UV lanes carry the whole effect. VISUALLY INERT marks a preset that rendered indistinguishably from the base and is neither W/A/U-only nor audio-driven — a finding, not a pass.

Generated offline: no ports bound, no live-engine writes, no network. Base capture via tools/pattern_audio_harness.mjs --synth silence.

Design Intent

Why This Exists / How It Differs

It provides the white family's fine-detail vocabulary without risking blackout or making every fixture sparkle equally.

Visual Goal on Titanic

Unique Topology

Distance Silhouette

The Silhouette carries a continuous white floor plus sparse stars, never a field of disconnected black gaps.

Close Detail

High-power Jewelry diamonds, crystalline Hull grain, fixture-cohort Organ stars, and fine sign frost create multiple scales of detail.

Motion Grammar

Counter-moving signed frost planes create large readable sweeps while deterministic facets and sparks move at subordinate rates; long wraps and parameter slew prevent seams and edit jumps.

XYZ Use

Centered X/Y/Z feed both deterministic hashes and the oblique frost bed; pixelLocalIndex authors the six-head Jewelry. Identity computes signIndex = index % 74 and remaps that folded address into one complete 10x8/74-pixel local surface; both physical 40+34 signs therefore use identical local coordinates.

Palette / White Material

No palette exports; warm-neutral RGB plus explicit W=A and U=0 makes the frost untintable and hardware-consistent.

Safety Floor

Each role owns a stable positive bed independent of density and sparkle phase; no sparse state can erase the whole ship.

Anti-Duplication

Keep it the sparse fine-detail member. Do not broaden glints into White Wash material, synchronize them into Breathe, line them up as Chase, or warm them into Temple candles.

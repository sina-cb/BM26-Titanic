White Shimmer

TOP · X/ZFRONT · X/YIDENTITY · TE SIGNS
0:00 / 0:10 Download GIF
TUNE

Visual conceptBroad crossing frost planes sweep through a complete white ship while deterministic facets and sparks reveal crystalline detail.

Design Intent

Replace low-resolution whole-rig glitter with legible crystalline motion: broad Hull sweeps, a protected counter-moving contour edge, hero Jewelry diamonds, restrained Organ cohorts, and a fully engraved Identity surface.

Why This Exists / How It Differs

It provides the white family's fine-detail vocabulary without risking blackout or making every fixture sparkle equally.

Visual Goal on Titanic

From playa distance the outline and signs remain stable; nearby, the rail diamonds should be the clearest and brightest native-white points over subtler frost elsewhere.

Unique Topology

Two broad oblique crystalline planes intersect over deterministic XYZ facets and point sparks; fixture-local six-head diamonds and a complete folded 74-pixel frost engraving refine the direct-view families.

Distance Silhouette

The Silhouette carries a continuous white floor plus sparse stars, never a field of disconnected black gaps.

Close Detail

High-power Jewelry diamonds, crystalline Hull grain, fixture-cohort Organ stars, and fine sign frost create multiple scales of detail.

Motion Grammar

Counter-moving signed frost planes create large readable sweeps while deterministic facets and sparks move at subordinate rates; long wraps and parameter slew prevent seams and edit jumps.

XYZ Use

Centered X/Y/Z feed both deterministic hashes and the oblique frost bed; pixelLocalIndex authors the six-head Jewelry. Identity computes signIndex = index % 74 and remaps that folded address into one complete 10x8/74-pixel local surface; both physical 40+34 signs therefore use identical local coordinates.

Palette / White Material

No palette exports; warm-neutral RGB plus explicit W=A and U=0 makes the frost untintable and hardware-consistent.

Safety Floor

Each role owns a stable positive bed independent of density and sparkle phase; no sparse state can erase the whole ship.

Anti-Duplication

Keep it the sparse fine-detail member. Do not broaden glints into White Wash material, synchronize them into Breathe, line them up as Chase, or warm them into Temple candles.

Fixture / instrument roles
  • Hull Canvas

    Low crystalline grain with distributed, restrained catchlights.

  • Silhouette

  • Jewelry

  • Organs

    Sparse structural stars phased by fixture identity.

  • Identity

    Both physical signs render the same complete, non-repeating 74-pixel frost plaque from signIndex=index%74, with fine shimmer over a stable full-surface floor.

  • TE signs

    Both physical signs render the same complete, non-repeating 74-pixel frost plaque from signIndex=index%74, with fine shimmer over a stable full-surface floor.

  • Jewelry white

    Jewelry gets exclusive hero treatment: the largest native-white accent rides crisp six-head diamonds while RGB maintains a visible rail between events.

White Chase

0:00 / 0:10 Download GIF
TUNE

Visual conceptA white procession crosses the ship in role-specific ribbons, tracers, pearls, strikes, and sign scans.

Design Intent

Retain a clear directional chase but stop projecting the same bar onto every fixture family; each instrument should express travel at its own scale.

Why This Exists / How It Differs

It is the white family's explicit motion look and the strongest manual-direction performance handle.

Visual Goal on Titanic

Broad Hull ribbons and a fine outline tracer should establish direction at distance, while six-head Jewelry marquee motion and discrete Organ hits provide close and structural authority.

Unique Topology

A fixed longitudinal X projection creates mirrored bow-to-stern processions; separate coordinates create a counter-moving Silhouette tracer, six-head Jewelry pearls, Organ cohorts, and one complete folded 74-pixel cross-scan.

Distance Silhouette

A narrower bright tracer travels across a permanently visible Silhouette, making both motion direction and ship outline legible.

Close Detail

Six-pearl rail marquees, fixture-cohort par strikes, broad Hull ribbon edges, and a restrained sign scan resolve at close range.

Motion Grammar

A manually signed long-wrap phase drives broad mirrored bow-to-stern travel; a counter-tracer and role-specific subordinate phases prevent lockstep repetition while controls slew without geometry jumps.

XYZ Use

Normalized longitudinal X establishes bow-to-stern travel while Y/Z preserve exact port-starboard mirroring and add vertical/depth facets; pixelLocalIndex builds Jewelry chases, and fixtureId builds Organ cohorts. Identity computes signIndex = index % 74 and remaps that folded address into one complete 10x8/74-pixel local surface; both physical 40+34 signs therefore use identical local coordinates.

Palette / White Material

No color palettes bind. RGB remains warm-neutral, W=A exactly, and UV=0 through every moving bar.

Safety Floor

Every instrument retains a white bed between processional cores; count, radius, and phase cannot create a whole-rig blackout.

Anti-Duplication

Protect explicit signed travel and multiple bar cores. It must not soften into Breathe, randomize into Shimmer, flatten into Wash, or slow into Temple's vigil.

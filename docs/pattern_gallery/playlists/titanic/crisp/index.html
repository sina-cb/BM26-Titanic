READY FOR OPERATOR

Visual conceptSolid endpoint-colored fireflies circle through the X/Z plane on wobbly deterministic orbits, leaving crisp finite echoes separated by black.

Design Intent

Build a deliberately non-blended visual language from solid circular bodies, exact palette endpoints, sharp antialiased edges, and strong black negative space.

Why This Exists / How It Differs

This prototype tests whether large readable orbital motion can remain chromatically discrete: no RGB interpolation, no feedback blur, and no incidental white or specialty channels.

Visual Goal on Titanic

At playa distance the Hull should read as several large moving colored discs cut from black, while the outline, stacks, pearls, and paired signs make the complete vessel legible.

Unique Topology

Five finite circular bodies use incommensurate cosine and sine wobble on normalized X/Z; each head may cast two smaller geometric echoes with explicit black gaps.

Distance Silhouette

Raw-LED strands emphasize narrow body rims and intersections so orbital geometry sharpens the recognizable ship outline instead of washing it into a field.

Close Detail

Antialiasing changes only the intensity of the winning endpoint color; overlaps use deterministic winner selection, and Jewelry contributes sparse exact-color pearls.

Motion Grammar

Every body advances smoothly along a deterministic orbit with irrationally related wobble frequencies; spacing fans their phases and trail adds finite earlier-phase echoes.

XYZ Use

Hull and Silhouette use normalized X/Z directly; Organs use local distance anchors, while Identity folds index modulo 74 into a complete paired 10x8 local sign surface.

Palette / White Material

Every lit RGB pixel is a scalar multiple of exactly Color 1 or exactly Color 2. Endpoints are never mixed with each other; black is the default material and W=A=U are always zero.

Safety Floor

The saved floor is zero for strong black negative space. Raising sliderSafetyFloor adds only a low exact-endpoint visibility bed, never gray or an interpolated hue.

Anti-Duplication

Keep the solid endpoint-only bodies, hard black gaps, and finite echoes distinct from 36_orbital_pulse, 118_grand_orbit_rings, and party_dancers/01; do not soften this into gradients, waves, or a continuous ring field.

Fixture / instrument roles
  • Hull Canvas

    Large filled X/Z circular bodies and finite separated echoes carry the primary composition.

  • Silhouette

    Narrow exact-color rims and deterministic intersection accents sharpen boundaries and the ship outline.

  • Jewelry

    Sparse moving exact-color pearls add nearby detail without white or generalized sparkle.

  • Organs

    Low steady endpoint anchors keep stacks and structural fixtures present beneath passing bodies.

  • Identity

    Both TE signs receive the same complete balanced 74-pixel local 2D orbit composition.

  • TE signs

    Both physical TE signs render the same balanced 74-pixel local orbit plaque using index modulo 74; addresses 40 through 73 continue the surface rather than repeating 0 through 33.

  • Jewelry white

    Jewelry uses sparse exact Color 1 or Color 2 pearls only; native white is deliberately absent and W=A=U remain zero.

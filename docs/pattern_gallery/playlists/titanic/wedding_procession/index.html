White Breathe

TUNE

Visual conceptOne slow white inhale moves through the ship as five coordinated but non-identical breaths.

Design Intent

Preserve the recognizable whole-rig breath while separating Hull volume, Silhouette crest, Jewelry pearls, Organ answers, and readable Identity.

Why This Exists / How It Differs

It is the family's soft organism: a welcoming white motion that reads as one body without a synchronized flat pulse.

Visual Goal on Titanic

The silhouette should stay visible through every exhale; Jewelry should reveal a sequential necklace inhale; Organs should answer the breath rather than merely copy its brightness.

Unique Topology

Two incommensurate scalar breaths combine with a signed oblique XYZ roll; role branches resample that envelope into physical breathing vocabularies.

Distance Silhouette

A travelling crest rides a continuous contour floor so the full vessel remains recognizable at the lowest point of the inhale.

Close Detail

Six-head pearl breathing, staggered par sources, Hull vertical volume, and gentle complete folded 74-pixel sign breath reward close viewing.

Motion Grammar

One signed clock reverses the whole breath and its quicker spatial roll; all phases use large seam-free wraps and controls slew smoothly.

XYZ Use

Centered X/Y/Z create the oblique rolling volume; pixelLocalIndex sequences Jewelry, and fixtureId offsets Organ breaths. Identity computes signIndex = index % 74 and remaps that folded address into one complete 10x8/74-pixel local surface; both physical 40+34 signs therefore use identical local coordinates.

Palette / White Material

No colorPalette exports; warm-neutral RGB, W=A byte-identical, and U=0 make every breath white and globally untintable.

Safety Floor

Every role has a positive bed before depth is applied; a full exhale remains a complete, readable ship.

Anti-Duplication

Protect the shared inhale grammar. It must not flatten into White Wash, fragment into Shimmer, travel as Chase bars, or become Temple's candle vigil.

  • Hull Canvas

    Broad luminous volume with a slow vertical lift.

  • Silhouette

    Continuous contour carrying the clearest travelling breath crest.

  • Jewelry

    Hero six-pearl necklace inhaling head by head with native-white cores.

  • Organs

    Staggered single-source answers to the shared inhale.

  • Identity

    Both physical signs render the same complete, non-repeating 74-pixel breathing nameplate from signIndex=index%74; the full surface inhales while retaining a protected floor.

  • TE signs

    Both physical signs render the same complete, non-repeating 74-pixel breathing nameplate from signIndex=index%74; the full surface inhales while retaining a protected floor.

  • Jewelry white

    Native white concentrates in the sequential pearl crests; the rail bed stays warm-neutral RGB so the six heads retain visible shape between crests.

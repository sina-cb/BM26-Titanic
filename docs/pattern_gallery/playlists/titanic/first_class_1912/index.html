Temple Warm White

TOP · X/ZFRONT · X/YIDENTITY · TE SIGNS
0:00 / 0:10 Download GIF
TUNE

Visual conceptA warm candle procession composes the vessel as layered wax and flame, lantern contour, Jewelry cups, Organ vigils, and engraved Identity fields.

Design Intent

Preserve Temple reverence and the hard ceiling while replacing equal warm dimness with a deliberate ceremonial hierarchy led by Vintage candle cups and a slow asymmetric procession fold.

Why This Exists / How It Differs

It is the white family's reverent slow look: warm, calm, readable, and intentionally incapable of becoming a party cue at its defaults.

Visual Goal on Titanic

The Hull should feel softly revealed through dust, the outline should remain lantern-clear, Jewelry should read as small candle sources, and Identity should be warm and still enough to read.

Unique Topology

Golden-ratio and square-root-related clocks shape layered candle wells, flame and wax material, a broad asymmetric procession fold, six candle cups, independent Organ vigils, and a complete folded 74-pixel field.

Distance Silhouette

A steady warm contour with a very shallow lift preserves the whole ship throughout the slow dust cycle.

Close Detail

Six-head candle flames, height-biased Organ vigils, fine Hull dust rays, and subtle complete folded 74-pixel warm-plaque variation create a ceremonial close read.

Motion Grammar

Very slow signed candle material and a broad asymmetric procession fold reverse together through large wraps; all controls slew and no pulse snaps or strobes.

XYZ Use

Centered X/Y/Z define candle wells, layered flame/wax material, and the broad procession fold; pixelLocalIndex authors Jewelry candles, while fixtureId phases Organ vigils. Identity computes signIndex = index % 74 and remaps that folded address into one complete 10x8/74-pixel local surface; both physical signs therefore use identical local coordinates.

Palette / White Material

No palette exports. Warmth reaches the family maximum R:G:B ratio of 1.00:0.68:0.32 at full control, W=A remains exact, UV is zero, and the ceiling binds RGB and W/A.

Safety Floor

The ceiling maps to a minimum 0.06 cap and every role owns a positive bed, so Temple can be dim but never black.

Anti-Duplication

Protect the candle-vigil restraint and hard ceiling. Do not brighten it into Wash, deepen it into Breathe, sharpen it into Shimmer, or turn it into a Chase.

Fixture / instrument roles
  • Hull Canvas

    Slow candle-white material and soft light-through-dust rays.

  • Silhouette

    Steady lantern contour with only a shallow warm lift.

  • Jewelry

    Hero row of six restrained candle cups with native-white flames.

  • Organs

    Quiet individual vigils shaped by fixture phase and real height.

  • Identity

    Both physical signs render the same complete, non-repeating 74-pixel warm vigil plaque from signIndex=index%74, with minimal full-surface lift and strong legibility.

  • TE signs

    Both physical signs render the same complete, non-repeating 74-pixel warm vigil plaque from signIndex=index%74, with minimal full-surface lift and strong legibility.

  • Jewelry white

    Vintage native white is limited to candle-flame cores; warm-neutral RGB carries the cup and halo so the Jewelry remains dimensional under the ceiling.

Suggested restrained audio mappings

    Shallow intensity (PRIMARY).

  • sliderKickKICK0..0.45 · pow2

    Gentle lift only.

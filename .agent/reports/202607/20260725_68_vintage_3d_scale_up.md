# 20260725_68 — Vintage lights: the 3D MODEL now renders at 2.5× (housing + pixels)

**Branch:** `feat/bm_readiness` · **Subsystem:** `simulation/` (3D render path only)
**Order (operator, verbatim, with a screenshot of tiny red dots by a davit):**
*"the vintage lights are still tiny in the 3d vis — please make sure they are
scaled up model-wise by 2.5X at at least (housing and pixels)."*

## TL;DR

Nothing in the sim ever drew a physically small fixture bigger than it is. A
Vintage LED Stage Light is **90 × 460 × 60 mm with six 18 mm heads on a 75 mm
pitch**, mounted on a ~100 m ship: at any normal camera distance the whole
fixture is a few specks. The previous vintage-sizing work (`_53`) could not have
fixed this — **it only ever bounded the bulbs DOWNWARD**.

Fixed with a per-fixture-type **render multiplier**: new
`simulation/src/fixtures/fixture_model_scale.js`, `VintageLed: 2.5`. It is
uniform, so housing, hitbox, head positions and bulb/halo radii all grow by the
same factor and the fixture's proportions are untouched. The **physical** pixel
positions are deliberately NOT scaled — the Pixelblaze/marsin model exporter and
the analytic light pool sample those, and an exported model must describe the
real rig, not the drawing of it.

| | before | after (2.5×) |
|---|---|---|
| housing box (drawn) | 90 × 460 × 60 mm | **225 × 1150 × 150 mm** |
| head spacing (drawn) | 75 mm | **187.5 mm** |
| bulb radius @ his slider (Global Pixel Size 1.1) | 0.022 | **0.055** |
| bulb radius @ slider max (5, pitch-clamped) | 0.0225 | **0.05625** |

## Root cause of "still tiny" — why `_53` didn't cover it

`_53` answered a different complaint (six heads fusing into one blob at high
slider values). Its whole mechanism is `clampPixelRadiusToPitch()` — a **ceiling**.
A ceiling can only ever make a bulb smaller; at the operator's own settings
(1.1 / 0.6) `_53` reported the vintage light as **pixel-identical** before and
after. So after `_53` the vintage light was exactly as small as it always was,
and at the slider max it was 4.4× *smaller* than before. Nothing regressed —
the "make it bigger" job simply had never been done.

Sizes came from **three places**, all now scaled together:

| what | where it came from |
|---|---|
| housing box + offset | `model_33.yaml` `shell.dimensions` / `shell.offset` (mm) |
| head positions | `model_33.yaml` `pixels[].dots` (mm) |
| bulb radius | `max(pixels[].size mm × 0.001, 0.02)` × "Global Pixel Size" |

`simulation/dmx/fixtures/vintage_led_stage_light/model_33.yaml` is the physical
truth of a real product and is generated by `gen_vintage.js`; `scenes/**` is
operator-owned. **Neither was touched.** The multiplier lives in the fixture
model code, exactly as briefed — no conflict to escalate.

## The clamp finding (this one nearly ate the whole change)

Vintage bulbs **do** go through `MAX_BULB_PITCH_FRACTION = 0.3`. The ceiling is
`pitch × 0.3`, and the pitch used to be measured in **physical** space
(0.075 ⇒ ceiling 0.0225). A 2.5× bulb (base 0.05) exceeds that ceiling at any
Global Pixel Size above **0.45** — so with the pitch left physical, the entire
scale-up would have been **silently clamped straight back to the old size** at
the operator's own 1.1, and he would have reported "still tiny" a second time.

Fix: `_minPixelPitch` is now measured on the **drawn** positions (`renderPos`),
so the ceiling scales with the fixture. At 2.5× the ceiling is 0.05625 — still
exactly 2.5× the old clamp, and `_53`'s anti-fusion guarantee is untouched
(drawn diameter 0.1125 against a drawn pitch of 0.1875). At render scale 1 the
pitch is byte-identical to before. `_53`'s whole suite still passes unmodified.

## Instancing (perf P0)

Preserved and asserted. A fixture is still **one `InstancedMesh` for bulbs and
one for halos**, `count === pixels.length`, matrices written through the same
`_instDummy` scratch — no new objects, no per-pixel meshes, no extra draw calls.
A test asserts `group.children.length <= 4` (shell + bulb batch + halo batch +
cones) so a future change can't quietly turn pixels back into scene-graph nodes.

## Changes

- **NEW `simulation/src/fixtures/fixture_model_scale.js`** — the one place that
  says "draw type X at N× physical size". Frozen table, validated at import
  (a non-positive entry **throws** at startup — no fallback), `fixtureModelScale()`
  returns 1 for any unlisted type.
- **`simulation/src/fixtures/dmx_fixture_runtime.js`** — `this._modelScale`
  applied to: fixture dimensions (hitbox + diffusor panel), shell geometry and
  shell offset, per-pixel `bulbSize` (hence `haloSize` / `haloBaseSize`), and a
  new per-pixel **`renderPos`** (= `localPos × scale`) used by every emitter
  matrix, cone matrix, LED diffusion sprite and the diffusor panel.
  `localPos` keeps its physical value for the exporter + light pool.
  The scale is applied **after** the 0.02 minimum-bulb floor, so "2.5× bigger"
  is true of the size actually drawn, not of a number the floor then eats.
- **NEW `simulation/tests/fixture_model_scale.test.js`** — 7 tests.

Zero writes to `scenes/**`, `models/**`, any fixture model YAML, or
`marsin_engine/**`. `pixel_map/**` and `gui_builder.js` untouched (agent `_66`'s
files) — this change is 3D-render-path only, which is also why the earlier 2D
pixel-map sizing work never moved the 3D view.

## Notes / follow-ups

- The multiplier is **per type and opt-in**. If the ShehdsBar or the pars read
  too small on the same rails, adding a line to `FIXTURE_MODEL_SCALE` is the
  whole change.
- `light_pool.js` still places its analytic spotlights at the **physical**
  pixel positions, so on a 2.5× vintage light a beam origin can sit up to
  ~0.3 m from the drawn head. Deliberate (real light placement stays honest);
  say the word if you'd rather the light followed the drawing.
- `DmxFixtureRuntime.getFixtureWidth/Height` (used only by scene generators for
  spacing) stay **physical** on purpose — they must not move where fixtures get
  generated.

# ADDENDUM — "Left Front Rails 4 still show small pixels"

**Operator follow-up:** *"Left Front Rails 4 still show small pixels — please

## Answer up front

**The scale IS global, and Left Front Rails 4 has it.** Measured off his own
running sim: all **16** VintageLed fixtures — front, back, port, starboard —
are drawn **identically**. What differs between the two fixtures he compared is
**brightness, not size**: the Left Front Rails are the **only patched vintage
lights in the scene**, so they render whatever the live DMX frame is sending
(at probe time two of the four were nearly black), while every other vintage
fixture — including all four Left Back Rails — is **unpatched** and therefore
sits at its full static config colour. At night in this sim a lit pixel's
apparent size is dominated by its additive halo and the bloom pass, so a dim
head shows only its 0.055 core and reads "small" beside a bright one.

**No code change was needed to make it global** (there is exactly one vintage
type and every instance already gets it). Two regression tests were added so it
stays that way, and the brightness question is handed back — see "For Sina".

## 1. What type is Left Front Rails 4 (and is it different from Left Back)?

Both are **`VintageLed`**, in the **same** `fixtures:` list of
`scenes/titanic/scene_config.yaml`, built by the **same** class. Their scene
entries are identical key-for-key apart from position/rotation:

| | Left Front Rails 4 | Left Back Rails 4 |
|---|---|---|
| `fixtureType` | `VintageLed` | `VintageLed` |
| section | `fixtures:` | `fixtures:` |

| `traceGenerated` / `enabled` / `brightness` | true / true / 100 | true / true / 100 |
| runtime class (live) | `DmxFixtureRuntime` | `DmxFixtureRuntime` |

No trace-generated-vs-hand-placed split, no legacy `ModelFixture` path (the
live page reports **0** fixtures in `dmxSceneFixtures`, so that path is not in
play on titanic at all), no second construction route.

**Registry audit** — 8 registered types (`ChauvetHaze4D`, `TEFogMachine`,
`ShehdsBar`, `TeLedGrid40`, `TeSignV3A40`, `TeSignV3B34`, `UkingPar`,
`VintageLed`). Exactly **one** is vintage-class; the other files in

### The 6 engine failures are environmental, not ours

Proven, not assumed. A detached worktree was created at clean `HEAD`
(`d091977b`) in the sibling `BM26-Titanic-worktrees/` dir, the two failing
files were run there, and they produced the **identical 6 failures**; the
worktree was then removed with `git worktree remove`.

- `tests/audio/audio_capture.test.js` × 5 — `AudioCapture` is constructed with a
  fake `spawnFn` but no `device`, and on Windows `resolveDevice()` throws
  `device_not_configured` ("Windows audio capture requires a pinned device").
  A platform gap in the test, not a regression.
- `tests/io/osc_listener.test.js` × 1 — expects `EADDRINUSE` on a double bind,
  the sandbox returns `bind EACCES 127.0.0.1:38317`.

Neither `audio/capture/audio_capture.js` nor `io/osc_listener.js` (nor their
tests) is modified on this branch — both were last touched in `c6eaa733` /
`f5d38e66`. Follow-up worth filing: pin a device in the audio_capture test
setup so the suite is green on Windows.

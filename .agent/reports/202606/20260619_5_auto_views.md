## View families generated

All derived purely from metadata the model ALREADY carries (group names,
`fixtureType`, per-pixel world `x/y/z`, `cId`). No exporter, WASM, or ABI
change. Every entry is `bit:0` (Tier-A).

| Family | Names | Source | Membership rep |
|---|---|---|---|
| **strand** (unchanged) | per-strand groups, `LEFT`, `RIGHT` | LED-strand group prefix (`deriveStrandViews`) | groups / pixelIndices |
| **spatial** | `PORT`, `STARBOARD`, `FORE`, `AFT` | `Left_/Right_` & `Front/Back` group tokens, **x/z sign cross-checked** | pixelIndices |
| **structural** | `WALLS`, `DECKS`, `CHIMNEYS`, `AUDITORIUM` | `Wall/Deck/Chimney/Auditorium` group token | groups (union) |
| **typed** | `@PAR`, `@BAR`, `@VINTAGE`, `@RAW` | `fixtureType` → canonical `FIX_*` role (`buildFixtureTypeIds`/`fixtureTypeId`) | pixelIndices |
| **band** | `BAND_LOW`, `BAND_MID`, `BAND_HIGH` | world `Y` quantized into thirds of the model's OWN Y extent | pixelIndices |
| **paired** | `<base>_BOTH` | union of a `Left`/`Right` group pair sharing a base name | groups (union) |
| **controller** | `CTRL_<cId>` | pixels per controller — only once patched (`cId != 0`) | pixelIndices |

### Naming convention (documented in `auto_views.js`)

- **Whole-ship spatial**: `PORT` / `STARBOARD` / `FORE` / `AFT`
  (UPPER, unambiguous ship terms; PORT/STARBOARD are the DMX+LED whole-ship
  counterpart to the LED-only `LEFT`/`RIGHT`).
- **Structural bands**: `WALLS` / `DECKS` / `CHIMNEYS` / `AUDITORIUM`
  (UPPER plural of the structure token).
- **Typed views**: `@`-prefixed (`@PAR`, `@BAR`, `@VINTAGE`, `@RAW`). The
  `@` prefix is **reserved for typed views** (report `20260618_2` §4.1) so
  they can NEVER collide with an authored or group name. The view name is
  the `FIX_*` role with `FIX_` dropped, the count suffix dropped, and a
  trailing `_LED` dropped (so `FIX_RAW_LED → @RAW`, `FIX_BAR_18 → @BAR`).
- **Vertical bands**: `BAND_LOW` / `BAND_MID` / `BAND_HIGH` (`BAND_` prefix,
  thirds of the model's own Y extent — model-agnostic, not a hard-coded
  threshold).
- **Symmetric composites**: `<base>_BOTH` (the side token stripped, `_BOTH`
  appended; only emitted when BOTH a Left and a Right group share the base).
- **Per-controller**: `CTRL_<cId>` (only when patched).

### Codex P0 — no silent empty masks, fail loud

- A family with **zero members registers NOTHING** (an empty-but-valid
  mask is a fallback). E.g. on a model with no Front/Back tokens, `FORE`/
  `AFT` simply do not appear; on an unpatched model `CTRL_*` does not
  appear (no `CTRL_0`).
- A genuine **model contradiction THROWS loudly**: if a pixel's group says
  `Left` (port) but its world `x` is positive (starboard), `deriveAutoViews`
  raises an error naming the pixel and the disagreement — a broken model,
  not a view to paper over. Pinned by a test.
- Names already owned by a base group / declared preset are **skipped**,
  and the internal `existing` set grows as entries register so families
  never collide with each other either. Pinned by tests.

## L/R naming normalization (audit §2.2)

Two DMX group pairs carried a trailing `Generator` on the Right side only,
breaking naive L/R pairing. The fixture `name` already included
"Generator"; only the `groupName` dropped it. Surgical fix — renamed the
two Left `groupName`s to match their Right counterparts, updated every
artifact that pins the group name:

- `simulation/scenes/titanic/scene_config.yaml`:
  `Left Center Auditorium → Left Center Auditorium Generator`,
  `Left Back Wall → Left Back Wall Generator` (the two `groupName:` fields).
- `simulation/scenes/titanic/views.yaml` — the two `groupBits` keys.
- `marsin_engine/models/titanic.viewmasks.js` — the two `groupBits` keys
  (bit assignments unchanged: `0x10`, `0x200`).
- `marsin_engine/models/titanic.js` — the `group:` field on the 90 + 7
  affected pixels (auto-generated model, exact-string update).

`patches.yaml` keys are fixture **names** (already "…Generator N"), so they
were untouched. The rig topology, bits, and pixel geometry are unchanged —
only the two cosmetic group-name strings.

# 2026-06-20 — FFT 1024→2048 + dependent-DSP re-tune (`dev/fft2048_retune`)

**Branch:** `dev/fft2048_retune` (off `feat/audio_analysis_2`). Worktree slot 0
(engine port 31068 / OSC 31000). The highest-leverage audio-quality follow-up
from the Round-2 backlog (`20260620_9` §BACKLOG, `20260620_11`): bump the analyzer
FFT 1024→2048 (keep `hopSize:512` → hop rate ~86 Hz unchanged) and re-validate +
re-tune every DSP block whose tuning depended on the old bin resolution.

## Why (the 1024 problem, from report 20260620_9)
At `fftSize:1024` / 44.1 kHz, **one bin = 43.07 Hz**:
- `audioNote`/`audioNoteHue` was quantization noise below ~110 Hz (bass roots
  mis-pitched).
- The `audio.sub` window (30–60 Hz) collapsed to a **single bin (bin 1, 43–86 Hz)
  that is a strict subset of the kick window** → `audioChestHit` was a near-
  duplicate of the kick, not the body-felt sub slam.

At `fftSize:2048`, **one bin = 21.53 Hz** — the resolution finally supports honest
sub-bass + bass-root pitch.

## BEFORE → AFTER numbers (real proof)

### 1. Dominant-freq accuracy + note pitch-class on known-pitch bass tones
Pure sustained tones, dom1 vs true freq, NoteEstimator pitch class (harness
`~/tmp/fft2048_measure.mjs`, run from `marsin_engine`):

| metric | 1024 | 2048 |
|---|---|---|
| mean \|dom1 − true\| over 8 bass roots (E1..G3) | **4.46 Hz** | **0.66 Hz** (6.7× better) |
| note pitch-class correct | **5/8** | **8/8** |

The roots that 1024 mangled (E1 41 Hz→read A#, A1 55 Hz→B, G2 98 Hz→G#) are all
correct at 2048. Per-tone error drops from up to 15.7 Hz (E1) to <1 Hz.

### 2. Sub-bass separation (the core 20260620_9 motivation)
`micSub` on a pure tone, harness `~/tmp/sub_sep.mjs`:

| tone | 1024 micSub | 2048 micSub |
|---|---|---|
| 40 Hz body-felt sub | 0.617 | 0.718 (sub fundamental captured) |
| **80 Hz kick-region** | **0.494** (sub ≈ kick) | **0.127** (sub no longer keys off the kick) |

At 2048 the sub window is bins 1–2 (≈21.5–64.6 Hz): bin 1 (21.5 Hz) sits BELOW the
kick. An 80 Hz kick that read 0.49 in the old single-bin sub now reads 0.13 — the
chest-hit is the body-felt sub slam, not a kick duplicate. Bin windows verified:
sub `[1,3)` vs kick `[2,5)` at 2048 (was sub `[1,2)` ⊂ kick `[1,3)` at 1024).

### 3. Drop detection (the re-tune)
`node tools/detection_eval.mjs` (labeled `detector_scenarios` × 3 mic tiers),
DETECTOR_DEFAULTS:

| config | drop P | R | F1 | tp/fp/fn | negFP / falseFiresPerMin |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1024, dropEnergyJump 1.8 (old default) | 1.00 | 0.556 | 0.714 | 5/0/4 | 0 / 0.00 |
| 2048, dropEnergyJump 1.8 (regressed) | 0.857 | 0.667 | 0.75 | 6/1/3 | 0 / 0.00 |
| **2048, dropEnergyJump 1.9 (shipped)** | **1.00** | **0.778** | **0.875** | **7/0/2** | **0 / 0.00** |

The 2048 bump alone improved recall (0.556→0.667) but produced one mistimed fire in
`full_arc@clean` (precision 1.00→0.857, tripping the `detector_eval.test.mjs`
`precision≥0.9` gate). PIVOT: swept the detector knobs — `dropMinLevel`/
`eventRefractoryMs` couldn't recover it without killing recall, but
**`dropEnergyJump 1.8→1.9`** holds the line and is strictly better than BOTH the
1024 baseline and 2048-default: **P=1.00, R=0.778, F1=0.875, zero phantom drops on
calm audio** (`falseFiresPerMin=0.00`, the dance-floor safety metric). The finer
spectrum sharpens the windowed ratio so genuine drops read a slightly larger jump.
Build (corr 0.97, peakErr −6 ms) and slow-zone (margin 0.65, acc 0.92) unchanged.

### 4. Per-hop perf (2048 is ~2× the transform)
`analyzer.pushSamples()` (one hop) over 4000 timed hops, mixed broadband, single-
threaded:

| | 1024 | 2048 | budget |
|---|---|---|---|
| mean | 0.073 ms | 0.149 ms | — |
| p99 | 0.174 ms | 0.279 ms | 0.5 ms/hop |

~2× the transform cost as expected, still comfortably under the 0.5 ms/hop budget.
`DerivedSignals.tick()` p99 = **0.41 ms** isolated (unchanged — it does no FFT).

## Genre validation (method + result)
No `tools/genre_eval.mjs` and `~/tmp/genre_corpus` is **empty** (real audio is
datacenter-gated, as prior reports note) → validated on the synth bank end-to-end:
drove the REAL analyzer (1024 vs 2048) + NoteEstimator into `GenreClassifier` on
genre-character synths and captured the internal feature vector + final genre
(`~/tmp/genre_validate.mjs`).

**Result: classification is INVARIANT across the FFT bump** — every case lands in
the same genre at 2048 as at 1024 (full_track→deep_house, kick_4floor→techno,
bassline→techno). Feature shifts are small/consistent (`sparkle` +0.05–0.07,
`lowMid` +0.02–0.03, `sparkleVar` +0.09–0.20 on melodic content, `melodic`/`bpm`
≈0 change), all sub-threshold for the profile match. **So the 1024-tuned profiles
still hold at 2048 — no profile retune is forced.** `tests/genre_classifier.test.js`
stays 9/9 green (it drives the classifier with synthetic raw signals → resolution-
independent, validates classifier LOGIC).

Pre-existing (NOT caused here, documented in `20260620_9`): `chord_progression`
reads techno at both sizes (`melodic≈0 ⇒ techno` over-selection). Unchanged by this
work. **Real-corpus genre re-tune (note-present flag, confSpread softmax) remains
the documented follow-up — needs labelled per-genre audio (gated).**

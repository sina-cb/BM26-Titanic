Notes

  • Active-low, internal pull-ups. Each button connects its GPIO straight to GND; firmware sets INPUT_PULLUP, so no external resistors are needed. Pressed = LOW.
  • Debounce. Firmware debounces in software (DEBOUNCE_MS = 15). An optional 100 nF cap across each switch (pin→GND) adds hardware filtering but is not required.
  • Power. 24 V → buck converter → 5 V into VSYS; share a common GND with the board and all switches. USB-C is for flashing/serial only — avoid back-feeding VBUS and VSYS at once.
  • Button lamps (×5). Each arcade lamp is on its own PWM GPIO (LAMP1–5 = GPIO 40/42/48/18/17), driving a low-side N-MOSFET gate (150 Ω series + 10 kΩ pulldown), with the lamps on a separate 5 V/12 V rail and a common ground. Recommended (future work): replace all five discrete drivers with one ULN2803A 8-channel board — amazon.com/…/B08C5B1S47 (wire GPIO→IN, lamp+→rail, lamp−→OUT, COM→+rail, GND→common; no gate R/pulldown needed).
  • Status LED is the module's onboard WS2812 on GPIO21 — already wired on the board.
  • Reserved pins — never assign a button here: 33–37 (octal PSRAM), 19/20 (native USB), 43/44 (UART0), 0/3/45/46 (strapping), 21 (onboard WS2812).

NOTE TRACKING

The colour note follows the bass-root anchor, but changes only after a new pitch has enough agreement and persists. Ambiguous noisy frames hold the current colour instead of flickering. The shipped timing usually responds around one 124 BPM beat and always leaves room inside a two-beat chord.

Ideal response ≈ 0.21 s far / 0.37 s near move at ~86 hops/s; measured ~0.44 s typical under noise.
